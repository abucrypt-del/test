Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

This policy covers Al Yazi Mandi's website (al-yazi.com), our staff billing/ordering system, and the associated Android, Windows, and Mac apps, all of which are operated by Al Yazi Mandi restaurant. It explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled.

Information from website visitors

When you reserve a cabin or table through al-yazi.com, we collect the name, phone number or email, date/time, and guest count you provide, solely to manage your reservation and contact you about it if needed.

Information within the staff app

The staff billing app (al-yazi.com/staff/billing) is an internal tool used only by Al Yazi Mandi employees to take orders, manage bookings, and process sales. It handles:

  • Staff accounts — name and role. Passwords are never stored in plain text; they are cryptographically hashed before being saved.
  • Guest and order information — name and phone number entered by staff for dine-in and takeaway orders, and reservations.
  • Sales records — items ordered, totals, and payment method, kept for the restaurant's own bookkeeping.

This information is used exclusively to run the restaurant's day-to-day operations and is never sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or other third parties.

The Android, Windows, and Mac apps

These apps are wrappers that display the same website described above — they do not collect any information beyond what's described here. They contain no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking software. The only permission they require is internet access, needed to load the site.

Where data is stored

Data is stored using Cloudflare's database infrastructure with industry-standard security practices. Access is restricted to authorized Al Yazi Mandi staff accounts.

Data retention and deletion

Reservation and order data is kept for as long as needed for the restaurant's operational and bookkeeping purposes. To request deletion of personal information you've provided, contact us using the details below.

Children's privacy

Our services are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised accordingly.
